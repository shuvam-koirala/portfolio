Privacy Policy for Palette Craft

Effective date: July 10, 2026

Palette Craft ("the app") is developed by Shuvam Koirala ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains how the app handles your information. In short: Palette Craft does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to us or to any third party.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any information. Palette Craft has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and no backend server. The app does not use the internet to send or receive data.

How the App Uses Device Permissions

  • Camera / Photo Library: Used only when you choose to take or pick a photo so the app can extract a color palette from it. Photos you select are processed entirely on your device and are never uploaded anywhere.
  • Storage (on-device database): Palettes and settings you create are saved locally on your device using an on-device database. This data never leaves your device and is deleted if you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • Share: If you use the "Share" feature, the app hands the selected content to the share sheet provided by your operating system, so you can send it to an app of your choice (e.g. Messages, Email). Palette Craft itself does not send this data anywhere.

Third-Party Services

Palette Craft does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. No data is shared with third parties because no data is collected in the first place.

Children's Privacy

Palette Craft does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. Since the app collects no personal data, it may be used by people of any age.

Data Retention & Deletion

All data created by the app (your saved palettes) stays only on your device. You can delete it at any time from within the app, or by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage from your device settings.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a revised effective date.

Contact Us
